# The Crucible - Roguelike Survival Crafter
## Gameplay: Craft weapons between waves to survive longer

### Core Loop
1. **Night Phase**: Enemies spawn, you fight with your current weapon
2. **Day Phase (30s)**: Craft/upgrade weapon at forge
3. **Repeat**: Waves get harder, survive as long as possible
4. **Death**: Record best wave, unlock new recipes

### Controls
- **WASD**: Move
- **Mouse**: Aim
- **Left Click**: Attack (automatic with equipped weapon)
- **Number Keys (1-3)**: Quick craft during day phase
- **ESC**: Pause

### Weapon Crafting
Each weapon is assembled from components:
- **Blade** (determines damage)
- **Handle** (determines attack speed)
- **Material** (determines durability)

### Strategy
- Light weapons = fast but weak
- Heavy weapons = slow but powerful
- Durability matters - weapons break after use!
