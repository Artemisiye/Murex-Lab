# Murex Lab: Crafting Stations & Infrastructure

Infrastructure items are built once and serve as permanent workstations within the Artificer's Lab.

## 🏺 The Primitive Workshop (Manual Labor)
### **Simple Fire Pit**
*   **Cost**: [Stone + Oak Log]
*   **Role**: Basic thermal processing. Unlocks Tallow rendering and simple Charcoal.

### **Drying Rack**
*   **Cost**: [Timber + Cordage]
*   **Role**: Moisture removal. Essential for turning Raw Hides into Dried Skins and herbs into powders.

### **Brick Mold (Tool)**
*   **Cost**: [Timber]
*   **Role**: Reusable tool. Although not a "station", it is the prerequisite for all masonry.

---

## 🧱 The Masonry Stage (Built Infrastructure)
### **Primitive Kiln**
*   **Cost**: [Stone + Unfired Bricks + Clay Paste]
*   **Role**: High-temperature masonry. Unlocks Fired Bricks, Pottery, and first-pass Charcoal.

### **Tannery Vat**
*   **Cost**: [Fired Bricks + River Water + Timber]
*   **Role**: Chemical immersion. Uses Tree Bark to turn Dried Skins into Cured Leather.

### **Simple Loom**
*   **Cost**: [Timber + Cordage + Stone Weights]
*   **Role**: Textile production. Weaves fibers into Fabric Bolts.

---

## 🔥 The Industrial Core (The Smeltery)
### **Artificer's Smelter**
*   **Cost**: [Fired Bricks + Mortar + Iron Reinforcement]
*   **Role**: Mineral extraction. Turns Ores into Ingots.
*   **Upgrade**: **Metallurgy Bellows** ([Timber + Cured Leather]). Required to reach temperatures for Steel.

### **Distillation Still**
*   **Cost**: [Copper Ingot + Glass Jar + Heat]
*   **Role**: Volatile extraction. Used to condense Quicksilver from Cinnabar and Essences from Herbs.

---

## ⚒️ The Artificer's Sanctum (The High Lab)
### **Iron Anvil**
*   **Cost**: [Iron Ingots + Rough Stone Base]
*   **Role**: Metal shaping. Unlocks complex weaponry and mechanical parts like Gears.

### **The Artificer’s Bench**
*   **Cost**: [Steel Ingot + Polished Timber + Copper Conduits]
*   **Role**: Complex assembly. The final station for combining mechanical, optical, and magical parts.

### **Garden Patch**
*   **Cost**: [Wooden Troughs + Garden Soil + River Water]
*   **Role**: Agricultural research. Allows for the sustainable growth of Bitter Herbs and exotic fog-flora.
